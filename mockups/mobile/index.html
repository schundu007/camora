Question asked
Tell me about a time you made a technical decision with incomplete information.
S
Sona's answer Reviewed
Use the STAR method. Ground your answer in a real constraint — time pressure, missing data, or team disagreement — then show your reasoning process clearly.
1
State the stakes — what would break if you chose wrong?
2
Show the reasoning — risk matrix, spike, lightweight prototype.
3
Quantify the result — latency, scale, team trust, time saved.

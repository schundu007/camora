# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Software engineers preparing for interviews and using live interview support,
study material, coding practice, job preparation, analytics, and interactive
technical playgrounds. They move between pages under time pressure and need
the same controls, status language, and navigation to remain predictable.

## Product Purpose

Camora helps engineers prepare deliberately and perform confidently by bringing
long-form study, coding practice, interview assistance, job preparation, and
hands-on labs into one product. Every page should make the next useful action
obvious without sacrificing depth or user control.

## Brand Personality

Clear, capable, calm. The interface should feel like a trusted professional
tool: focused, structured, and quietly confident rather than playful or noisy.
Azure-inspired Fluent clarity should carry across the entire application,
including Lumora, Capra, jobs, playground, analytics, settings, and auth.

## Anti-references

Avoid disconnected page-specific themes, dense dashboard clutter, decorative
glassmorphism, generic SaaS card grids, low-contrast gray text, tiny labels,
and motion that competes with study or interview work.

## Design Principles

- One product system: shared shell, tokens, controls, states, and interaction
  language across every route.
- Make hierarchy visible: users should understand location, status, and the
  next action at a glance.
- Calm under pressure: reduce decoration and preserve readable, predictable
  workflows on time-sensitive screens.
- Respect expertise: expose useful detail progressively instead of hiding it
  behind ornamental chrome.
- Consistency is a feature: equivalent actions should look and behave alike.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access and visible focus, readable
contrast in both themes, pointer targets of at least 44px for primary
controls, reduced-motion support, and text labels that do not rely on color
alone.
